# nacre roadmap

This roadmap defines completion through machine-checkable evidence. A milestone is not complete because its implementation exists; it is complete only when every exit criterion passes on the applicable committed corpus.

Effort estimates are rough guesses for one experienced full-time-equivalent developer. They exclude waiting for upstream changes and can be wrong by a factor of two or more. M0--M2 are engineering milestones with documented algorithms and reference implementations to study. M3 and M4 are research-grade; their estimates carry substantially more uncertainty.

## M0 — Foundation

**Risk:** Engineering. The main risk is choosing a data layout or contract that makes later topology work unsafe or expensive.

**Guess:** 3--5 weeks.

### Scope

- Maintain the two-distribution `uv` workspace and license boundary.
- Freeze the CSR layout, fields, dtypes, and OpenFOAM ordering conventions of the structure-of-arrays, face-based `PolyMeshIR`.
- Deliberately defer `SurfaceInput` until M1, when the Gmsh bake pipeline provides its first real producer.
- Implement the OpenFOAM `polyMesh` writer.
- Write invariant checkers before the corresponding implementation.
- Build a versioned corpus of 20--30 source geometries from clean analytic cases through hostile CAD, with manifests and reference metadata. Baked `SurfaceInput` `.npz` files begin in M1.
- Give hostile inputs with gaps or self-intersections an independently defined watertight target and reference volume, so volume checks remain meaningful.
- Build deterministic PNG rendering into `tests/artifacts/` and archive it per commit.
- Establish CI for structural checks, Hypothesis, artifact generation, and `checkMesh`.
- Make JAX x64 activation and rejection of float32 core arrays import/test gates.

### Exit criteria

- A hand-constructed polyhedral cube round-trips through `PolyMeshIR` without changing point coordinates, face vertex cycles, owners, neighbours, patches, or cell volume beyond `1e-12` absolute tolerance.
- Its exported `polyMesh` passes OpenFOAM `checkMesh` with zero errors.
- Structural checkers report: one owner per face, exactly two adjacent cells per internal face, no duplicate canonical faces, `owner < neighbour`, positive cell volume, and area-weighted face-normal closure residual at most `1e-12` times total cell-face area.
- The complete Hypothesis suite for `meshir.py` and its checkers is green with its random seed recorded by CI on failure.
- Every committed M0 geometry has a manifest, expected outcome, reference scale, and deterministic PNG artifact.
- A CI test fails if JAX x64 is inactive or any core floating-point array is float32.

## M1 — Size field

**Risk:** Engineering. BREP sign conventions, feature proximity, and stable blending require care, but the numerical components are well understood.

**Guess:** 4--6 weeks.

### Scope

- Define and freeze `SurfaceInput` and `SizeField` against the Gmsh bake producer before implementing size-field work.
- Bake and commit the corpus `.npz` golden files so core CI no longer requires Gmsh.
- Import principal curvature from Gmsh BREP queries during baking.
- Add body-of-influence (BOI) sizing through explicit geometry primitives.
- Add proximity sizing through signed-distance queries.
- Blend sources into a positive size field with an explicit Lipschitz/growth-rate limit.
- Use JAX for batched fixed-shape evaluation and NumPy for topology and persistence.

### Exit criteria

- On analytic spheres at three radii spanning two orders of magnitude, both absolute principal curvatures match `1/R` with maximum relative error at most 1% away from declared parametrization singularities.
- On analytic plane, sphere, cylinder, and box queries, SDF values match analytic distances with absolute error at most `1e-8` times the geometry reference length.
- For every adjacent sample pair in the fixed grid suite and every pair generated by Hypothesis, `|h(x)-h(y)| <= g ||x-y|| + 1e-12 L`, where `g` is the configured growth limit and `L` is the case reference length.
- Inside each BOI primitive's declared constant-size interior, the returned size matches the request within `max(1e-12 L, 1e-6 h_requested)`.
- The size-field checker rejects NaN, infinity, non-positive size, and growth-bound violations.

## M2 — Octree core

**Release:** **v0.1**, a usable octree-core CFD mesher.

**Risk:** Engineering, with robustness risk concentrated in classification and trimming. The algorithms are documented and references exist.

**Guess:** 8--14 weeks.

M2 alone is a shippable, useful tool. The project has practical value before boundary-layer extrusion or conformal transition exists.

### Scope

- Construct and refine the octree against the unified size field.
- Enforce 2:1 balance.
- Classify cells inside and outside the target volume.
- Trim boundary cells into valid face-based polyhedra.
- Begin classification and trimming alongside octree work if useful, but split them into `trim.py` before `octree.py` approaches the 800-line budget. This is planned structure, not an opportunistic cross-module refactor.
- Grow `optimize.py` with the quality operations needed by core and trimmed cells.

### Exit criteria

- The octree checker finds zero face-adjacent leaf pairs whose refinement levels differ by more than one.
- Every one of the 20--30 corpus geometries, including cases with declared dirty input surfaces, produces a `polyMesh` that passes `checkMesh` with zero errors.
- Every produced cell passes face-sharing, closure, uniqueness, orientation, and positive-volume structural checks.
- Total mesh volume differs from each case's independently stored reference volume by at most 1%.
- Refining every size request by a factor of two reduces the volume discrepancy or leaves it below the 1% threshold on every corpus case.
- CI reports all fixed cross-milestone metrics for every corpus case and the aggregate corpus failure rate is 0% at release.

## M3 — Boundary-layer extrusion

**Release:** **v0.2**, adding the standalone anisotropic boundary-layer extruder.

**Risk:** Research-grade. Robust multi-normal extrusion, collision handling, and local termination on hostile geometry require a literature review and a precise algorithmic specification before implementation.

**Guess:** 16--28 weeks.

### Scope

- Progress from single-normal extrusion to multi-normal treatment at features.
- Detect local and non-local collisions.
- Support per-face layer termination and shrinking without invalid topology.
- Preserve patch identities and produce a cap that can later be stitched conformally.
- Grow `optimize.py` with layer-specific quality objectives and constrained smoothing.

### Exit criteria

- The extrusion checker reports zero self-intersecting or non-positive-volume layer cells and zero non-manifold layer faces across the full corpus.
- Requested layer thickness is monotonic at every non-terminated wall column, within `1e-10` times the local total layer height.
- Every mesh-producing extrusion case exports and passes `checkMesh` with zero errors.
- In the laminar flat-plate solver case, the velocity-profile L2 relative error against Blasius is at most 5% and local skin-friction coefficient error is at most 5% over the declared comparison interval.
- At least 95% of covered wall faces achieve the configured target y+ within 20%; excluded and terminated faces are reported separately.
- Layer coverage rate is reported for every corpus case. No unreported face exclusion is permitted; the release does not impose a universal minimum coverage threshold because legitimate termination depends on geometry.
- All seven cross-milestone metrics are emitted in the release report.

## M4 — Conformal polyhedral transition

**Release:** **v1.0**, delivering the distinguishing complete workflow: boundary-layer cap to octree core through a conformal polyhedral transition.

**Risk:** Research-grade and the highest-risk milestone. There is no clean open-source reference implementation for the intended transition. Work begins with literature review, explicit topology cases, and a machine-checkable algorithmic specification—not generated implementation code.

**Guess:** 20--40 weeks.

### Scope

- Specify and implement a conformal transition between an anisotropic boundary-layer cap and the trimmed octree core.
- Handle refinement changes, layer termination, feature junctions, and mixed polygonal faces without hanging or duplicate interfaces.
- Grow `optimize.py` with transition-cell objectives and constrained smoothing.
- Complete OpenFOAM, CGNS `NGON_n`/`NFACE_n`, and VTU output paths for the v1 workflow.

### Exit criteria

- The stitch checker reports zero unmatched, overlapping, or multiply owned interface faces; every transition face is represented identically on the boundary-layer and core sides after orientation normalization.
- Every transition cell has positive volume, satisfies closure, and belongs to a single connected valid volume mesh.
- Every corpus geometry completes the full pipeline and passes `checkMesh` with zero errors; corpus failure rate is 0%.
- Nightly and release validation passes all numeric gates: flat-plate profile and skin friction within 5%; cylinder Re=40 drag coefficient and recirculation length within 10% of references; backward-facing-step Re=800 reattachment length within 10%; and curved-duct surface-resolution error within its pre-registered 5% reference tolerance.
- CGNS connectivity round-trips without changing face-node cycles or cell-face incidence, and VTU reports the same point and cell counts as `PolyMeshIR`.
- All seven cross-milestone metrics are emitted and archived for v1.0.

## Cross-cutting work

`optimize.py` grows incrementally with every milestone rather than arriving as a final cleanup pass. Each objective must have a checker-visible quality effect, fixed-shape JAX kernels where appropriate, and a regression case. Documentation and the gallery are updated at every release from archived artifacts and measured results only.

The solver cases are release gates as soon as their required mesh capability exists. Reference values, sampling windows, OpenFOAM version, solver settings, and tolerances are committed before evaluating a release candidate.

## Fixed tracked metrics

Every mesh-producing milestone reports the same columns. Values are stored per case and summarized per commit so trends remain comparable across project history.

| Metric | Fixed definition |
|---|---|
| Corpus failure rate | Failed corpus cases divided by attempted cases; a failure is a pipeline error, structural-check failure, `checkMesh` error, or missed milestone-specific numeric gate. |
| Max non-orthogonality | Maximum face non-orthogonality in degrees, using the value reported by `checkMesh`. |
| Max skewness | Maximum cell/face skewness reported by `checkMesh`. |
| Max aspect ratio | Maximum cell aspect ratio reported by `checkMesh`; the OpenFOAM version is recorded with the result. |
| Layer coverage rate | Wall area covered by the requested layer stack divided by eligible requested wall area; `N/A` before M3. |
| Cell count | Number of volume cells in the exported mesh. |
| Wall time | End-to-end meshing elapsed wall-clock seconds, excluding bake time, on a named reference machine and fixed worker count. |

Robustness is the headline: corpus failure rate takes precedence over isolated best-case quality. Quality and timing metrics are tracked, not promoted as claims until enough reproducible history exists.
